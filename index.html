Welcome to Sunrise

A place built for NEET & JEE aspirants.

Preparation years are hard enough — your stay shouldn't be. Sunrise Hostels keeps the essentials effortless: four filling veg meals a day, quiet study spaces, a library, spotless rooms and a gate that opens straight onto Gurukripa Coaching. You focus on the rank; we handle the rest.

K Karni Ram · Founder
Our story

It started with one building and a promise.

“A Unique Group of Sunrise.”

Founded in 2020 by Karni Ram, Sunrise grew from a single hostel into a four-wing group — two wings for girls, two for boys — without ever changing the idea it began with: run the place like the students' own families are watching. Every renewal and every referral since traces back to that promise.

Questions

Asked often, answered honestly.

What is included in the stay?

Everything listed above — four pure-veg meals a day, WiFi, housekeeping, hot water, RO drinking water, laundry access, study hall, library and all utilities. The tariff we agree on is the tariff you pay.

What are the room tariffs?

Tariffs depend on wing and room type (single or two seater) — every room across the campus is fully air-conditioned. Visit us or message on WhatsApp — we'll show you the rooms and discuss the best rate, including any ongoing offers.

Can parents visit before booking?

Absolutely — we encourage it. Walk in any day or call ahead on 96021 14011 and we'll give you the full tour of the wing you're interested in.

How do payments work?

All payment methods are accepted — UPI, cash, card and bank transfer.
